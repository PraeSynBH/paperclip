## How to Complete Training

1. Read each module in sequence (Modules 1 → 2 → 3).
2. After each module, take the knowledge check quiz in `training/assessments/`:
   - Module 1 quiz: `training/assessments/module1-quiz.md`
   - Module 2 quiz: `training/assessments/module2-quiz.md`
   - Module 3 quiz: `training/assessments/module3-quiz.md`
3. Complete the comprehensive assessment: `training/assessments/assessment1-comprehensive.md`
4. Record your completion by notifying the Awareness Agent or updating `training/records/attendance.csv`.

## Completion Criteria

You are marked **completed** when:
- All three modules (MOD-1, MOD-2, MOD-3) are done
- Assessment (ASSESS-1) passed with ≥ 70%
- Policy acknowledgment signed

## Policy Acknowledgment

All personnel must acknowledge the following ISMS policies:
- Information Security Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-001)
- Access Control Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-002)
- Supplier Management Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-003)
- Incident Management Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-004)
- Asset Management Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-005)
- Acceptable Use Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-006)
- Change Management Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-007)
- Business Continuity Policy (AIS-ISMS-POL-008)

Policies are located in `policies/iso27001/`. See the policy index at `policies/iso27001/POLICY-INDEX.md`.

## For Managers

If you have direct reports:
- Ensure your team members are allocated time for training.
- Track completion and follow up with overdue team members.
- Include a security topic in at least one team meeting per month.
