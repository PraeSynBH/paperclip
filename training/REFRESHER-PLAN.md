# Annual Refresher Training Plan

**Programme:** Aira ISMS Awareness & Training Programme  
**Version:** 1.0  
**First Refresher Target:** July 2027  
**ISO Controls:** A.6.3 (ongoing), A.5.4 (management reinforcement)  

---

## Purpose

Annual refresher training ensures that all personnel maintain current awareness of security threats, policies, and their responsibilities. It satisfies the ISO 27001:2022 requirement for "regular updates" under A.6.3.

## Schedule

| Event | Timing | Audience | Duration |
|-------|--------|----------|----------|
| Annual Refresher Session | July each year | All Personnel | 45 min |
| Management Security Briefing | July each year | Managers | 30 min (additional) |
| Policy Update Acknowledgment | As policies change | Affected Personnel | 15 min |
| Supplemental Threat Briefing | As needed (incidents) | Relevant Teams | 15 min |

## Refresher Content Template

### Section 1: What Has Changed (15 min)

- Policy updates since last training cycle
- New controls or procedures introduced
- Changes to regulatory or contractual obligations
- New tools or systems with security implications

### Section 2: Threat Landscape Update (10 min)

- Top 3 threats observed in the past year (sourced from SecOps)
- Industry trends relevant to Aira's operations
- Real incident examples (anonymized, from Aira or industry)
- Updated phishing and social engineering tactics

### Section 3: Lessons Learned (10 min)

- Security incidents that occurred (anonymized)
- Root causes and preventative actions taken
- What personnel should have done differently
- Positive examples: incidents that were caught due to good reporting

### Section 4: Knowledge Refresh & Quiz (10 min)

- Quick knowledge check on core concepts
- Discussion of common misconceptions
- Q&A opportunity

## Success Metrics (Annual)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Completion rate | >= 95% of active personnel |
| Quiz pass rate | >= 85% |
| Policy acknowledgment rate | 100% |
| Satisfaction score | >= 4/5 (optional survey) |

## Phishing Simulation (Conditional on CISO Approval)

If authorized by the CISO, annual refresher training may include:

- Pre-training baseline phishing simulation (no real credential capture)
- Post-training phishing simulation (6 weeks after)
- Metrics: click rate, report rate, time-to-report
- Goal: measurable reduction in click rate and increase in report rate
- **Requires explicit CISO scope approval before launching**

## Reinforcement Cadence

Between annual refreshers, maintain awareness through:

| Frequency | Activity | Duration |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Monthly | Micro-learning email/tip | 2 min read |
| Quarterly | Manager security talking points | 5 min in team meeting |
| Bi-annual | Simulated phishing exercise (if approved) | Automated |
| Ad-hoc | Threat-specific alerts (critical vulnerabilities) | Brief notification |

## Recurring Review

The refresher programme itself is reviewed annually for:
- Content relevance to current threats
- Effectiveness (trend in metrics)
- Audience feedback
- Alignment with ISO 27001 requirement updates
