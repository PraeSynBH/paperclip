## 1. Introduction to Aira Security Policies

Aira's security policies define the rules and expectations for protecting information assets. These policies are part of the ISMS and are mandatory for all personnel.

### Policy Hierarchy

```
Information Security Policy (top-level)
  ├── Acceptable Use Policy
  ├── Access Control Policy
  ├── Data Classification Policy
  ├── Incident Response Policy
  ├── Supplier Security Policy
  └── Topic-Specific Policies
```

## 2. Acceptable Use Policy Summary

### Computing Resources

- Aira-provided devices and systems are for business use.
- Limited personal use is permitted if it does not:
  - Interfere with work duties
  - Consume excessive resources
  - Violate any other policy
  - Introduce security risks

### Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal content
- Harassing, threatening, or discriminatory behaviour
- Unauthorized access to systems or data (even if you have technical ability)
- Installing unapproved software without authorization
- Disabling or bypassing security controls (antivirus, firewall, updates)
- Sharing credentials or accounts
- Connecting unauthorized devices to Aira networks
- Using unapproved cloud services for storing Aira data
- Circumventing web filtering or monitoring controls

### Email and Communications

- Aira email is for business communication.
- Be cautious with attachments and links from unknown senders.
- Do not forward chain emails or mass communications without approval.
- Report phishing attempts immediately.
- Do not send sensitive data via unencrypted email.

### Internet Usage

- Internet access is provided for business purposes.
- Web filtering is in place to block known malicious sites.
- Do not attempt to bypass web filtering.
- Be mindful of what you download — only from trusted sources.

## 3. Data Classification & Handling

Aira classifies data into three levels:

| Classification | Description | Examples | Handling |
|----------------|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Public** | Approved for public release | Marketing materials, public docs | No special restrictions |
| **Internal** | For Aira personnel only | Internal communications, project docs | Not to be shared externally |
| **Confidential** | Sensitive; restricted access | Customer data, credentials, designs | Encryption, access control, audit logging |

### Handling Rules

- **Confidential data**: Encrypt at rest and in transit. Never store on personal devices. Log all access.
- **Internal data**: Share only with personnel who need it for their work.
- **Public data**: Must be approved before release.

### Clean Desk and Clear Screen

- Lock your screen when stepping away from your workstation.
- Store physical documents with confidential information in locked cabinets.
- Shred confidential paper documents when no longer needed.
- Do not leave sensitive information visible on whiteboards or monitors.

## 4. Access Control Policy Summary

### Principles

- **Least Privilege**: You receive the minimum access needed to perform your job.
- **Need to Know**: Access to confidential data requires a specific business need.
- **Separation of Duties**: Critical actions require more than one person.

### Password Requirements

- Minimum 12 characters
- Use a password manager (recommended)
- Unique passwords for each service
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever available
- Never share your password
- Report suspected credential compromise immediately

### Access Reviews

- Managers review team access rights quarterly.
- Access is revoked promptly when someone leaves or changes roles.
- Privileged access (admin, root) is reviewed monthly.

## 5. Incident Response Policy Summary

### What is a Security Incident?

- Suspected or confirmed data breach
- Malware infection
- Unauthorized access
- Lost or stolen device
- Phishing attack (even if you didn't click)
- Policy violation that could lead to a breach

### Your Responsibilities

1. **Report immediately** — do not wait or try to handle it alone.
2. **Do not destroy evidence** — preserve logs, emails, files.
3. **Do not discuss publicly** — keep incident details confidential during investigation.
4. **Cooperate with investigators** — provide accurate information when asked.

### Reporting Channels

- Primary: Report to your manager and the security team
- Anonymous: [to be configured per project]
- Urgent (suspected active breach): Escalate to CISO directly

## 6. Supplier Security Policy Summary

- All third-party suppliers who access Aira data or systems must undergo security review.
- Suppliers must meet Aira's security requirements.
- Supplier access is time-bound and reviewed regularly.
- Report any supplier security concerns to the security team.

## 7. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Policy violations may result in:
- Additional mandatory training
- Temporary access suspension
- Disciplinary action (per HR policy)
- Legal action (for criminal violations)

## 8. Policy Acknowledgment

All personnel must acknowledge they have read and understood Aira's security policies. This acknowledgment is recorded and auditable.
