## 1. What is ISO 27001?

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). It provides a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information so that it remains secure.

### Key Concepts

- **CIA Triad**: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability — the three pillars of information security.
  - *Confidentiality*: Information is not disclosed to unauthorized individuals.
  - *Integrity*: Information is accurate and complete, and has not been tampered with.
  - *Availability*: Information is accessible when needed by authorized users.

- **ISMS**: A set of policies, procedures, processes, and systems that manage information security risks.

- **Risk-Based Approach**: ISO 27001 is built on identifying risks and applying controls proportional to those risks.

- **Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)**: The continuous improvement cycle that underpins the ISMS.

### Why ISO 27001 Matters for Aira

- Builds trust with customers and partners
- Provides a structured defence against security threats
- Ensures regulatory compliance
- Demonstrates security maturity to auditors and stakeholders
- Protects Aira's intellectual property and customer data

## 2. The ISO 27001:2022 Control Framework

ISO 27001:2022 Annex A defines 93 controls organized into 4 themes:

| Theme | Focus | Examples |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Organizational** (A.5) | Policies, roles, responsibilities | A.5.4 Management responsibilities |
| **People** (A.6) | Personnel security | A.6.3 Awareness & training |
| **Physical** (A.7) | Facilities, equipment | A.7.1 Physical security perimeter |
| **Technological** (A.8) | IT systems, networks | A.8.8 Vulnerability management |

### Control A.6.3 in Detail

> "Personnel of the organization and relevant interested parties shall receive appropriate information security awareness education and training and regular updates of the organizational information security policy and procedures, as relevant for their job function."

**What this means for you:**
- You will receive security awareness training when you join and annually thereafter.
- You are expected to understand and follow Aira's security policies.
- You must report security incidents and concerns promptly.

### Control A.5.4 in Detail

> "Management shall require all personnel to apply information security in accordance with the established information security policy and topic-specific policies of the organization."

**What this means for managers:**
- You must ensure your team completes training.
- You must model good security behaviour.
- You are responsible for enforcing security policies within your team.

## 3. Your Role in Information Security

Security is everyone's responsibility — not just the security team's.

### All Personnel

- Complete assigned security awareness training
- Read and acknowledge Aira security policies
- Report suspicious activity, phishing attempts, and security incidents
- Handle data according to its classification level
- Lock your workstation when away
- Use strong, unique passwords and a password manager
- Do not share accounts or credentials

### Managers

- Ensure team members complete training on time
- Conduct security discussions in team meetings
- Review access rights for team members regularly
- Report security gaps or concerns to the CISO

## 4. The Aira ISMS in Practice

### Continuous Monitoring

Aira's platform provides continuous compliance monitoring. This means:
- Controls are tested regularly, not just before audits.
- Evidence is collected automatically where possible.
- The ISMS is always audit-ready.

### Incident Reporting

If you notice a security issue:
1. Report it immediately to your manager or the security team.
2. Do not attempt to fix or hide security problems.
3. Preserve evidence — do not delete or modify affected systems.
4. Use the incident reporting channel: [to be defined per project].

## 5. Summary

- ISO 27001 provides a framework for protecting Aira's information.
- The ISMS is a living system — it requires everyone's participation.
- Security awareness training is mandatory and repeated.
- You are the first line of defence.
