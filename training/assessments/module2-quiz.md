# Module 2 Knowledge Check: ISMS Policies & Acceptable Use

**Quiz ID:** MOD2-QUIZ
**Covers:** Module 2 — Aira ISMS Policies & Acceptable Use
**Questions:** 10 multiple choice
**Pass Mark:** 70% (7/10)
**Time:** 10 minutes

---

## Questions

### Q1. Which of the following sits at the top of Aira's policy hierarchy?

A) Acceptable Use Policy
B) Access Control Policy
C) Information Security Policy
D) Incident Response Policy

### Q2. Under the Acceptable Use Policy, limited personal use of Aira devices is:

A) Strictly prohibited under all circumstances
B) Permitted if it does not interfere with work duties or introduce security risks
C) Permitted for any activity
D) Only permitted outside working hours

### Q3. Which of the following is explicitly prohibited under the Acceptable Use Policy?

A) Using a password manager
B) Reporting a security incident
C) Sharing credentials or accounts
D) Locking your workstation

### Q4. Aira classifies data into three levels. Which of the following is the correct classification for customer data?

A) Public
B) Internal
C) Confidential
D) Unclassified

### Q5. What is the minimum password length required by Aira's access control policy?

A) 8 characters
B) 10 characters
C) 12 characters
D) 16 characters

### Q6. The principle of "Least Privilege" means:

A) All employees have the same access rights
B) Only the IT department has system access
C) You receive the minimum access needed to perform your job
D) Access is reviewed annually

### Q7. How should you handle confidential data when it is no longer needed?

A) Keep it indefinitely in case it's needed later
B) Delete or securely dispose of it per the retention policy
C) Move it to a personal device for safekeeping
D) Email it to yourself for backup

### Q8. Which of the following IS a security incident that must be reported?

A) A colleague forgetting their password
B) A phishing email received (even if you didn't click it)
C) A slow internet connection
D) A routine software update notification

### Q9. When you report a security incident, you should:

A) Delete all related emails and files to clean up
B) Discuss it on social media to warn others
C) Preserve evidence and not discuss publicly during investigation
D) Try to identify and punish the person responsible

### Q10. What is required after reading and understanding Aira's security policies?

A) Nothing — reading is sufficient
B) A signed policy acknowledgment, which is recorded and auditable
C) A verbal confirmation to your manager
D) A security certification exam
