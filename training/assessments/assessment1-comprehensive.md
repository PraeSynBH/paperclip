# Effectiveness Assessment: ISO 27001 Awareness & Training

**Assessment ID:** ASSESS-1  
**Covers:** Modules 1, 2, and 3  
**Pass Mark:** 70% (14 out of 20 correct)  
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes  
**Format:** Multiple choice

## Instructions

Answer all 20 questions. Each question has one correct answer unless stated otherwise.  
Mark your answers on the answer sheet.  
This is not punitive — the purpose is to verify understanding and identify knowledge gaps.

## Section A: ISO 27001 Fundamentals (Questions 1–7)

### Q1. What does the "C" in the CIA triad stand for?

A) Compliance  
B) Confidentiality  
C) Continuity  
D) Control  

### Q2. What does an ISMS stand for?

A) Information Security Monitoring System  
B) Internal Security Management Standard  
C) Information Security Management System  
D) Internet Security Monitoring Service

A) 2  
B) 3  
C) 4  
D) 5

B) Plan, Do, Check, Act  
C) Prepare, Deploy, Control, Audit  
D) Plan, Deliver, Confirm, Adjust

B) The IT department only  
C) Everyone  
D) External auditors
