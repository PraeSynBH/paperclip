# Month 1: Phishing Awareness

**Deliver:** August 2026 (first Monday)
**Time to read:** 2 minutes

## Headline: Think Before You Click

Phishing is still the number one way attackers get into organisations. One wrong click can compromise your account, our data, and our customers' trust.

## Why It Matters

Over 90% of security breaches start with a phishing email. Attackers impersonate colleagues, vendors, or services you trust. They create urgency ("your account will be deleted!") or curiosity ("see the attached invoice") to bypass your judgment.

## What To Do

- **Hover before you click.** Mouse over links to see where they really go. If the address looks wrong or unfamiliar, don't click.
- **Check the sender.** Is the email from someone you know? Is the domain correct (not "gmaill.com" instead of "gmail.com")? When in doubt, contact the sender through a different channel (Slack, phone).
- **Never share credentials or codes.** No legitimate IT team or service will email you asking for your password, MFA code, or to "verify your account" via a link.

## Quick Check

When was the last time you hovered over a link before clicking it?

## Report It

If you receive a suspicious email, report it immediately. Forward it to your security team or use the "Report Phishing" button if available. Reporting helps protect everyone — you might be the first to spot a new attack.
