# Month 7: Secure Remote Work

**Deliver:** February 2027 (first Monday)
**Time to read:** 2 minutes
**ISO Controls:** A.6.7, A.8.16

---

## Headline: Your Home Office Is Now Part of Our Security Perimeter

Working remotely doesn't mean working with less security. Your network, your devices, and your environment all matter.

## Why It Matters

Remote and hybrid work expand the attack surface. Home Wi-Fi routers are rarely patched. Family members may access your work device. Conversations in co-working spaces can be overheard. Attackers specifically target remote workers because they're outside the office's physical and network protections.

## What To Do

- **Use the VPN for all work traffic.** No exceptions. The VPN encrypts your connection so attackers on the same cafe Wi-Fi cannot see what you're doing. Connect before accessing any work systems.
- **Secure your home network.** Change the default admin password on your router. Enable WPA3 or WPA2 encryption. Keep your router's firmware updated. Consider a separate guest network for IoT devices.
- **Keep work and personal separate.** Don't let family members use your work laptop. Don't access personal email or social media from a device connected to sensitive work systems. If you must use a personal device, use the approved BYOD container or VDI.

## Quick Check

Is your home Wi-Fi router still using the default password it came with?

## Report It

If you notice unusual activity on your work device while working remotely (unexpected pop-ups, new software you didn't install, cursor moving on its own), disconnect from the network and report it immediately.
