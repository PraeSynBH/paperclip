# Month 4: Social Engineering

**Subject:** [Security Awareness] Social Engineering
**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Audience:** All Arrowhead/Aira Personnel

---

**Headline:** Hackers Don't Always Hack Computers — Sometimes They Hack People

**Why it matters:** Social engineering bypasses technical controls by manipulating people. Attackers may impersonate Aira vendors (Drata support, Google Cloud rep), new team members, or even executives. They exploit helpfulness, fear of authority, and urgency to get what they want — access tokens, configuration details, or direct system access.

**What to do:**
1. Verify unexpected requests through a second channel — call the person back on a known number, not one they provide.
2. Be especially cautious of requests to bypass normal procedures, share credentials, or install remote access software.
3. "I need to verify this with my manager" is always an acceptable answer.

**Quick check:** If someone called you claiming to be from "Google Cloud support" and asked for your API key, what would you do?

**Report it:** Report any unsolicited request for sensitive information, even if you didn't fall for it. Your report protects the next person.
