# Month 10: Data Disposal

**Subject:** [Security Awareness] Data Disposal
**Date:** 2027-05-01
**Audience:** All Arrowhead/Aira Personnel

---

**Headline:** Deleting Isn't Enough — Dispose of Data the Right Way

**Why it matters:** Deleted files, old emails, and decommissioned devices can contain years of Aira configuration data, client information, and audit records. Simply dragging to the trash or formatting a drive doesn't actually destroy the data. ISO 27001 requires secure disposal procedures — and it applies to both digital and physical media.

**What to do:**
1. Use secure deletion tools for digital files; for paper, use a cross-cut shredder rated P-4 or higher.
2. Before returning or decommissioning a company device, follow the offboarding checklist to wipe it properly.
3. Review and purge old files quarterly — don't hoard sensitive data "just in case."

**Quick check:** When was the last time you securely deleted a file containing Aira configuration data?

**Report it:** If you find old hardware (USB drives, hard drives, old phones) with company data, report it for secure disposal — don't just throw it away.
