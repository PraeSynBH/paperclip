# Month 1: Phishing Awareness

**Subject:** [Security Awareness] Phishing Awareness
**Date:** 2026-08-01
**Audience:** All Arrowhead/Aira Personnel

---

**Headline:** Think Before You Click — Phishing Is Still the #1 Entry Point

**Why it matters:** 90% of data breaches start with a phishing email. Attackers impersonate trusted services like Drata, Google, or internal IT to trick you into revealing credentials or installing malware. One click can compromise Aira's entire ISMS.

**What to do:**
1. Check the sender's actual email address — hover over the name, don't trust the display name.
2. Never enter credentials after clicking a link in an unsolicited email. Navigate to the service directly.
3. Look for urgency or fear ("act now," "your account will be deleted") — these are red flags.

**Quick check:** Think of the last unexpected email you received. Did you check the sender address or just the sender name?

**Report it:** Forward suspicious emails to your IT/security contact. Reporting is a strength, not an embarrassment.
