# Month 7: Secure Remote Work

**Subject:** [Security Awareness] Secure Remote Work
**Date:** 2027-02-01
**Audience:** All Arrowhead/Aira Personnel

---

**Headline:** Your Home Office Is Part of Aira's Security Perimeter

**Why it matters:** Remote and hybrid work extends the security boundary to coffee shops, co-working spaces, and home networks. Public Wi-Fi, shared devices, and shoulder-surfing create risks that don't exist in the office. Aira's Drata integration and Google AI access are only as secure as the environment you connect from.

**What to do:**
1. Always use the company VPN when connecting to Aira systems from outside the office.
2. Never work on sensitive Aira data from public Wi-Fi without a VPN — use your phone hotspot if needed.
3. Keep work on work devices. Don't access Aira systems from personal laptops, tablets, or shared family computers.

**Quick check:** When was the last time you connected to Aira systems from outside your home? Were you on a secure network?

**Report it:** If you suspect someone saw your screen while working in public, or if your work device is lost/stolen, report it within the hour.
