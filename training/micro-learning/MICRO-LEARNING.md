# Micro-Learning Reinforcement Programme

**Programme:** Aira ISMS Awareness & Training Programme
**Component:** Monthly Micro-Learning (Spaced Reinforcement)
**Version:** 1.0
**Created:** July 2026
**ISO Controls:** A.6.3 (training), A.5.4 (management responsibilities)
**Project:** Aira: Drata and Google AI Integration

---

## Purpose

Short, repeated security awareness touches delivered monthly between annual refresher sessions. Each tip takes approximately 2 minutes to read and is designed for spaced reinforcement — keeping security top-of-mind without overwhelming personnel.

## Audience

All Arrowhead/Aira personnel with access to company systems and data. Includes employees, contractors, and anyone who completed the ISMS fundamentals training.

## Delivery Mechanism

**Primary:** Email distribution via the programme manager or IT. Each monthly tip is a self-contained, plain-text/markdown email.

**Optional Enhancements (future):**
- Post in company chat/Teams channel for discussion
- Manager talking points for team meeting reference
- Quarterly round-up quiz (every 3 months, 5 questions)

## Content Template

Each monthly tip follows this structure:

1. **Headline** — a catchy, memorable phrase (1 line)
2. **Why it matters** — one or two sentences tying to real risk (2-3 lines)
3. **What to do** — three actionable bullets (3 bullets)
4. **Quick check** — one self-reflection question
5. **Report it** — reminder to report suspicious activity
6. **Footer** — programme name, date, contact

## Distribution Instructions

1. Send on the **first Monday** of each month
2. Subject line format: `[Security Awareness] <Headline>`
3. Send from: security@aira or IT distribution address
4. BCC all recipients (protect distribution list privacy)
5. Archive sent copies in `training/micro-learning/sent/`

## Reinforcement Loop

- After every 3 tips, send a 5-question quiz covering the last 3 topics
- Track quiz results; revisit topics with <70% pass rate
- Feed common knowledge gaps back into annual refresher content
- If SecOps reports a real incident tied to a topic, re-send that topic's tip as a "real-world reminder"
