# Month 6: Incident Reporting

**Deliver:** January 2027 (first Monday)
**Time to read:** 2 minutes
**ISO Control:** A.6.8

---

## Headline: If You See Something, Say Something — Immediately

The difference between a minor incident and a major breach is often how quickly it's reported. Minutes matter.

## Why It Matters

Organisations that detect and contain a breach within 200 days save an average of $1 million compared to those that take longer. The longer an incident goes unreported, the more damage it does. Even a "probably nothing" report can be the early warning that prevents a crisis.

## What To Do

- **Know what to report.** Suspicious emails, unusual system behaviour, lost devices, unauthorised access, data accidentally sent to the wrong person, policy violations you witness — if it feels wrong, report it.
- **Know how to report.** Use the designated reporting channel: email to security@aira, the incident report form, or notify your manager. Don't assume someone else already reported it.
- **Don't investigate on your own.** Unless you're on the security team, don't try to "check if it's really a problem" by clicking links, running files, or probing. Report first, let the experts assess.

## Quick Check

Do you know exactly how to report a security concern right now, without looking it up? If not, find out today.

## Report It
