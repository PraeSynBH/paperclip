# Month 3: Clean Desk & Clear Screen

**Deliver:** October 2026 (first Monday)
**Time to read:** 2 minutes
**ISO Control:** A.7.1

---

## Headline: If They Can See It, They Can Steal It

Physical security is cybersecurity. A sticky note with a password, a whiteboard full of architecture diagrams, or an unlocked laptop in a coffee shop — all are breaches waiting to happen.

## Why It Matters

Shoulder surfing, office visitors, cleaning staff, and lost devices are real threats. A single photo of a whiteboard or a left-behind printed document can expose sensitive customer data, credentials, or intellectual property. Physical breaches don't leave digital footprints — you may never know it happened.

## What To Do

- **Clear your desk at the end of the day.** Store documents, notebooks, and removable media in a locked drawer or cabinet. Shred sensitive papers — don't just bin them.
- **Lock your screen every time you step away.** Train the habit: `Win+L` (Windows) or `Ctrl+Cmd+Q` (Mac). Set auto-lock to 5 minutes or less.
- **Never leave devices unattended in public.** A laptop left on a cafe table for 30 seconds is 30 seconds too long. Take it with you or lock it with a cable lock.

## Quick Check

Look at your desk right now. Is there anything a visitor could read that they shouldn't?

## Report It

If you see unattended sensitive material — an unlocked screen, a printed document left out — close it, cover it, or notify the person. If you lose a device, report it immediately so access can be revoked.
