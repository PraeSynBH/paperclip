## Overview

Product outreach starts NOW, not after v0.5.0. Talking to potential customers while building informs what to build.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)

### Case Studies — Voyonder as Customer Zero

Voyonder Travel is our own dogfood testbed. We are the reference implementation.

| # | Case Study | Focus | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How Voyonder runs its own company with AI employees | Full operations overview — hiring agents, assigning work, review cycles, budget management | COO | Week 2 |
| 2 | From concept to production: Building Paperclip while using Paperclip | Meta case study on dogfooding — how we use our own product to build the product | COO | Week 3 |
| 3 | AI agent governance at Voyonder | Reviews, approvals, budgets, and audit trails in practice | COO | Week 3 |

### Quickstart Guide (VOY-1334)

Assigned to Support Engineer. A 5-minute "from zero to running your first AI company" guide.

### Beta Customer Recruitment

The CEO will recruit 5 beta customers from their network. Target profiles:
- Travel agencies interested in AI concierge services
- SaaS companies evaluating AI employee platforms
- Ops teams looking for autonomous AI workers with governance
- Small startups needing AI team augmentation

## Phase 2: Launch (Week 2-3)

### Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Owner | Launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discord community | Early adopter engagement, support, feedback | COO | Week 2 |
| Technical blog | "How we run an AI company with AI employees" | CTO | Week 2 |
| "Paperclip for X" landing pages | Use-case-specific landing pages (travel agency, CPA, support) | CEO | Week 3 |
| Demo video | "Create your own AI travel concierge in 10 minutes" | Founding Engineer | Week 3 |

### Beta Program

- 5 beta customers from founder's network
- Weekly check-in calls
- Structured feedback collection
- NPS survey at 30 days

## Phase 3: Scale (v0.5.0+)

### Pricing Research

Research competitors: e2b, Modal, Replit Teams, Cursor. Determine:
- Pricing tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise)
- Usage-based vs seat-based billing
- Free tier limits
- Annual discount structure

### Marketing Assets

- Product screenshots and demo GIFs
- Customer testimonial collection
- Comparison pages vs alternatives
- Integrations page
