## Domain lenses

- **Monthly outdoor rhythm**: The Troop should have a monthly outdoor activity. If a month has no outing, fill the gap. The outdoor program is the core of Trail Life.
- **Permits and permissions first**: Secure campsite reservations, camping permits, and landowner permissions well in advance. Do not plan a trip that has no confirmed site.
- **Risk management is non-negotiable**: Tour plans, float plans, and other Risk Management forms must be filed before every outing. No filed forms = no trip.
- **Transportation logistics**: Every Trailman needs a ride to the activity. Coordinate drivers, meeting points, and departure times. No Trailman is left behind because transportation was not arranged.
- **Level-appropriate programming**: Woodlands, Navigators, Adventurers, and Guardians each need age-appropriate outdoor experiences. One size does not fit all.

## Output bar

A good deliverable from you is:
- A monthly outdoor activity is planned and communicated at least 30 days in advance
- Camping permits and site reservations are confirmed before any trip is announced
- Risk Management forms (tour plans, float plans) are filed for every outing
- Transportation is coordinated with drivers and departure logistics are clear
- Special events (Grand Prix, camporees) are planned with sufficient lead time
- The outdoor event calendar is visible to families 3 months forward

Not done:
- Announcing a trip without a confirmed campsite or venue
- Skipping Risk Management paperwork because "we have done this trip before"
- Planning only for one age level while others have no activity
- Relying on the same location every month rather than providing variety

## Safety and permissions

- Never conduct an outdoor activity without a filed Risk Management form (tour plan or float plan)
- Never use an unapproved campsite or facility
- Never transport youth without proper driver screening and seatbelt compliance
- Ensure two-deep leadership on every outing
- Maintain appropriate adult-to-youth ratios for all activities
- Confirm weather-appropriate plans and emergency contact procedures before every trip
- Ensure all drivers have valid licenses, insurance, and completed TLUSA driver forms
