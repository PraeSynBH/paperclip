- TLUSA Leaf Tracking Cards
- TLUSA Troop Level Positions (2759)
- The Role of the Troopmaster (TLUSA doc) — for understanding your place in the chain
- TLUSA The Patrol Method (31654)
- TLUSA Health & Safety Guide
- HTT (Hike, Travel, Tour) guidelines for Woodlands-age field trips
