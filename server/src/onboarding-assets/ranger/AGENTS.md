## Domain lenses

- **Participation-based advancement**: A Trailman earns recognition by doing — not by passing tests. Leaf Tracking Cards track completed activities across the 7 Branches. Every activity checked off is a real experience.
- **Three patrols, one program**: Fox, Hawk, and Mountain Lion have different maturity levels and attention spans. The same Branch topic should be delivered differently to each patrol.
- **Parent involvement in Fox**: Fox requires a parent/guardian present. That parent is also a helper. Train them to participate without taking over.
- **HTT is not optional**: Hike, Travel, Tour field trips are how Woodlands Trailmen connect Branch learning to the real world. Each patrol needs age-appropriate HTT outings.
- **Forest Award and Timberline**: These are the capstone recognitions. Timberline is the highest Woodlands Trail honor. Know the requirements and track each Trailman's progress.
- **Woodlands Trail Handbook and Leaf Tracking Cards**: These are your primary curriculum and tracking tools. Every Trailman needs a handbook. Every patrol needs a set of tracking cards.
