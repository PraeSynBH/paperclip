# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** AIS-ISMS-POL-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** [Pending ISMS Owner approval]
**Review Cycle:** Annual or upon significant change
**Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the overarching information security management system (ISMS) for Rambur, Inc. and defines management commitment, roles, and the framework for all topic-specific security policies.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Rambur personnel, contractors, and third parties who access or handle Rambur information assets. It covers all information systems, networks, applications, and data regardless of location or hosting model.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 ISMS Establishment (A.5.1)

Rambur shall establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an ISMS in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The ISMS shall be documented, communicated, and available to all relevant interested parties.

### 3.2 Management Direction (A.5.1, A.5.4)

Senior management shall:
- Approve this policy and all topic-specific policies
- Allocate adequate resources for ISMS implementation and maintenance
- Communicate the importance of effective information security management
- Ensure ISMS objectives are established and aligned with business objectives
- Conduct management reviews of the ISMS at planned intervals, no less than annually

### 3.3 Policy Framework (A.5.1)

This policy is the parent document for all topic-specific policies. All subsidiary policies must:
- Align with this policy's objectives
- Map to applicable ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls
- Be reviewed and approved by the ISMS Owner
- Be reviewed at least annually

### 3.4 Roles and Responsibilities (A.5.2)

The following ISMS roles are established:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| ISMS Owner | Overall ISMS accountability, policy approval, resource allocation |
| CISO | Security strategy, risk management, compliance oversight |
| Engineering Lead | Technical control implementation, secure development |
| Compliance Agent | Control mapping, audit readiness, evidence collection |
| All Personnel | Adhere to policies, report security events, complete training |

### 3.5 Segregation of Duties (A.5.3)

Conflicting duties and areas of responsibility shall be segregated to reduce the risk of error or fraud. No single individual shall have unrestricted ability to:
- Initiate, approve, and implement changes to production systems
- Authorize and process financial transactions involving security tooling
- Bypass security controls without compensating controls and documented approval

### 3.6 Policy Communication (A.5.1, A.5.4)

This policy and all topic-specific policies shall be:
- Published in a centrally accessible repository
- Communicated to all personnel upon hire and upon each revision
- Acknowledged by all personnel in writing or electronic equivalent
- Made available to relevant external parties upon request

### 3.7 Compliance and Enforcement (A.5.1, A.6.4)

- Compliance with this policy is mandatory
- Violations shall be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination
- The disciplinary process is documented in the HR policy framework
- Exceptions require documented risk acceptance signed by the ISMS Owner

### 3.8 Policy Review (A.5.1, A.5.36)

- This policy shall be reviewed at least annually by the ISMS Owner
- Review shall consider: changes in business context, threat landscape, regulatory requirements, audit findings, and incident lessons learned
- Review outcomes shall be documented in management review minutes
